Schools

Primary and secondary teaching positions across CBSE, ICSE, State Board and international curricula.

  • Subject teachers, PGT/TGT/PRT roles
  • Coordinators and vice-principals
  • Qualification, board experience shown upfront

Category 2

Intermediate

Junior college and pre-university lecturers for MPC, BiPC, CEC and commerce streams.

  • PG faculty with subject specialisation
  • Entrance-exam coaching experience valued
  • Full and part-time openings

How Upadyay helps each side

A faster, more accurate way to reach the right hire — for the person looking, and the institution looking for them.

1

Build a real profile

Faculty enter qualification, specialisation, publications, patents and experience — once, verified, reusable everywhere.

2

Get matched by category

Employers search within School, Intermediate or Higher Ed, filtered by subject, qualification and experience.

3

Institutions post roles

Employers list openings and reach faculty directly from a database of 18,000+ profiles — no listing fee for the first two years.

4

Connect and hire

Shortlist, message and close the role from one dashboard, with every credential already verified.
