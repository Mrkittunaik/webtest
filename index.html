Information

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

Elevator Types & Applications

We offer panoramic, home, MRL, hospital, freight, capsule, and fully customized elevators — each suited to a different building type, from private villas to hospitals and warehouses.

Technical Specifications

Key specs to plan around include cabin capacity (kg), travel speed (m/s), number of floors served, and shaft/pit dimensions — our team confirms exact figures during a site visit.

Safety & Compliance

All installations follow relevant national elevator safety codes, with certified components, emergency backup systems, and regular safety inspections.

Installation Process

Installation typically runs through site survey, shaft preparation, equipment delivery, mechanical and electrical fit-out, testing, and final commissioning.

Interior Materials & Customization

Choose from stainless steel, glass, wood, and decorative wall panels, plus stone, marble, or vinyl flooring — our design team helps you finalize a combination that fits your space.

Maintenance & Support

We offer scheduled maintenance contracts with routine inspections, priority breakdown response, and genuine spare parts to keep your elevator running smoothly.
